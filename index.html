My name is Vanessa and I am a UX/UI designer and accessibility specialist based in Italy, open to relocation across the EU. I design accessible, user-centred and scalable digital experiences. I believe good design starts with deeply understanding people, users, stakeholders and teammates, to craft solutions that are effortless and cognitively inclusive. My work in numbers:

6 platforms designed
12+ WCAG audits
1500+ findings
7+ clients in banking, insurance, gov

I have been working at SMC (DGS Group) since January 2024, designing web platforms for public administration, banking, insurance and B2B clients, where accessibility and compliance are not optional. I translate complex requirements into interfaces that balance business goals and user needs, and I run WCAG audits on what we and others ship. I use AI tools every day for heuristic reviews, wireframe ideation and realistic placeholder content, which leaves more time for the decisions that need a human.

I learnt to code during my bachelor's and master's degrees, so I speak the developers' language and spot technical constraints early, from the first wireframe to the handoff.

My core skills include:

  • User research (interviews, surveys, usability testing)
  • Information architecture & user flows
  • Wireframing and high-fidelity prototyping
  • Design systems & component libraries
  • Visual design and UI patterns
  • Accessibility audits (WCAG 2.1/2.2, EN 301 549, screen-reader testing with NVDA, JAWS, VoiceOver, TalkBack)
  • Responsive design and developer handoff
  • AI-assisted design workflow

My career goal is to work in a multidisciplinary team where strategy, craft and technology meet to build products that people actually enjoy using, and to keep growing as a designer who makes accessibility part of quality, not an afterthought.

Outside work I draw, on paper and digitally, shoot and edit short videos, play and record music, and I am currently learning photographic composition with film cameras. Also, in my spare time I like to unwind by watching a good movie and crocheting. Thanks for reading and have a good day, Vanessa!
